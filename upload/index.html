Company Overview

An AI-driven technology, engineering and international trading company focused on developing, integrating and implementing intelligent systems.

Who We Are

Epigater Solutions is an AI-driven technology, engineering and international trading company focused on developing, integrating and implementing intelligent systems that help organizations operate more efficiently, securely and sustainably.

The company designs and delivers AI-enhanced software applications, enterprise resource planning systems, business automation platforms, mobile applications, cloud solutions, cybersecurity systems, data infrastructure, smart energy technologies, industrial automation solutions and integrated digital platforms.

An Integrated Technology Ecosystem

Unlike conventional technology companies that focus on a single product or service, Epigater operates as an integrated technology ecosystem. The company develops proprietary software, customizes international platforms, integrates infrastructure, supplies technological products and provides implementation and support services.

Epigater combines artificial intelligence, software engineering, infrastructure integration, smart energy, industrial technology and international trade under one coordinated corporate structure. This enables the company to provide complete solutions—from business analysis, system design and software development to equipment sourcing, implementation, training, maintenance and long-term technical support.

What We Deliver

Epigater's solutions are designed to improve productivity, reduce operational costs, strengthen institutional control, increase transparency, enhance customer experience, improve energy efficiency and support evidence-based decision-making.

AI-Enhanced Software

Smart Energy

Solar PV, battery storage, microgrids, smart metering, EV charging.

Smart Manufacturing

Industrial automation, computer vision, predictive maintenance, digital twins.

Global Trade

Import, export, distribution of technology, energy and industrial products.

Who We Serve

The company serves government institutions, financial organizations, manufacturers, energy companies, healthcare institutions, educational organizations, telecommunications operators, retailers, logistics companies, construction companies, development organizations and other public and private enterprises.

Epigater develops digital representations of physical systems, equipment, and facilities to support performance simulation, capacity analysis, maintenance planning, energy optimization, operational testing, failure analysis, and process redesign.
